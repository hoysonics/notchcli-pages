# NotchCli Support

NotchCli shows Claude Code and Codex CLI quota in a panel attached to the top of your Mac display.

## Open the panel

Hover over or click the hardware notch. Clicking pins the panel open. On a Mac without a notch, use the compact panel at the top edge of the display.

## Allow log access

Open Settings from the panel footer, then choose your `.claude` and `.codex` folders. NotchCli requests read-only access and stores the permission in the macOS App Sandbox.

## Missing quota data

Codex quota is read from local Codex CLI session logs. Claude quota requires the locally installed `claude` command or a compatible local quota snapshot. If a source is unavailable, its card explains what is missing.
